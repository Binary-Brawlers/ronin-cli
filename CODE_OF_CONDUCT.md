# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus criticism on ideas and code, assume good intent, and respect maintainers' decisions and community boundaries. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
